About College

Jaya Jagannath Degree College was established in 2023 with a visionary mission to bring accessible, high-quality higher education to the youth of Jeypore and its surrounding regions in Odisha. Named after Lord Jagannath, the institution embodies core values of community development, inclusive education, and moral growth.

The college operates as a recognized, private un-aided institution affiliated with Vikram Dev University (a prominent State Public University in Odisha). Located strategically on Bypass Road near the Power House Colony (N.H. 326), the college began its journey by introducing comprehensive undergraduate and higher secondary programs across diverse fields including B.Sc CS and BCA.
